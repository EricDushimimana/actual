Accounting & Financial

Bookkeeping Services

ACTUAL ACCOUNTING provides meticulous bookkeeping to maintain accurate financial ledgers for businesses in Rwanda. We handle daily transaction recording, vendor invoicing, bank reconciliations, and payroll entries.

Key Deliverables:

  • Monthly general ledger maintenance and account balancing
  • Bank statement reconciliations and petty cash management
  • Accounts payable and receivable tracking
Accounting & Financial

Tax Preparation & Compliance

Navigating tax laws in Rwanda requires strict compliance with Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) directives. We handle Value Added Tax (VAT) declarations, Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) withholdings, and Corporate Income Tax (CIT) filings.

Key Deliverables:

  • Monthly VAT return computation and EBM invoice auditing
  • PAYE, RSSB pension compliance, and statutory tax filings
  • Annual corporate tax return preparation and audit assistance
Accounting & Financial

Financial Reporting

Clear financial reporting gives enterprise leaders a crystal-clear understanding of profitability and liquidity. We compile balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow projections formatted to international standards.

Key Deliverables:

  • Quarterly Balance Sheet & Income Statement generation
  • Cash flow analysis and working capital forecasting
  • Management reporting packs for board members and investors
